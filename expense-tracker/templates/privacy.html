Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the Service, we store the expense records, categories, amounts, and dates you enter in order to provide you with spending summaries and history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to operate and improve the Service — for example, to display your expense history, generate spending breakdowns, and maintain your account. We do not sell your personal data or expense records to third parties, and we do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are accessible only through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You may access, update, or delete the expense data associated with your account at any time through the Service. You may also request that your account and associated data be permanently deleted by contacting us. Closing your account will remove your data from active use in the Service.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Service or for legal and regulatory reasons. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after any changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
